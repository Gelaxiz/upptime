# Gelaxiz Status Design

The status page is a calm network-operations ledger: a near-black canvas, thin graphite rules, cool-gray type, and small, unambiguous status signals. It belongs to the same world as gelaxiz.xyz while being denser and more operational.

## Hierarchy

The navigation is a compact utility strip. The opening title is large but restrained, followed by incidents and the live service ledger. Service names lead; uptime and response values form a compact secondary reading; graphs stay visible as quiet evidence rather than card decoration.

## Typography

Use Archivo when available, falling back to the system sans stack. Headings are compact, slightly tightened, and high contrast. Operational labels use uppercase with measured tracking. Numeric readings use tabular figures.

## Geometry and spacing

The content width is 1120 px. Surfaces use 8–12 px radii and one-pixel rules, never heavy shadows. Desktop services read as a continuous ledger; mobile rows retain 16 px internal spacing and controls remain touch-safe.

## Motion

The page enters with a short opacity and vertical reveal. Service rows may reveal in sequence, and interactive surfaces use 140–180 ms color/border transitions. Motion never delays status information. Under reduced motion, transforms and entrance sequences are removed.
